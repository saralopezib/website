¡Hola! I'm a designer, researcher and facilitator who loves untangling complexity - whether that's a service, a product, or something as personal as someone's understanding of their own mental health.

That instinct started early. At 17 I went through a difficult year with my own mental health, and years later turned that experience into Mindnosis - a mental health self-assessment kit that was featured by Dezeen and won a Creative Conscience Award. It led to several years of research and creative facilitation work with organisations including Mind in Camden, London Brain Project, and NHS trusts across London, exploring how design can support recovery, participation and communication.

The same human-centred approach carried into a decade in product, UX and service design: helping local authorities tackle wicked issues like homelessness and social care with FutureGov and Uscreates, and more recently six years as Lead Designer at Kaluza, leading UX design and research across a global climate tech SaaS platform.

I'm now bringing both strands back together as independent work: UX consulting, and creative workshops for wellbeing. Different problems, same way of working.

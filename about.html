Hi there, I'm Sara Lopez

I'm a product designer who spends most of my energy untangling complexity for people who need it simplified — whether that's a payments system or someone's understanding of their own mental health.

That combination started with a personal project. At 17 I went through a difficult year with my own mental health, and years later I turned that experience into Mindnosis — a mental health self-assessment kit that won a Creative Conscience Award and was featured by Dezeen. It led to several years of research and creative facilitation work with organisations including Mind in Camden, NSUN, and NHS trusts across London, exploring how design can support recovery, participation, and communication.

That same human-centred instinct carried straight into a decade in product and service design: helping local authorities tackle homelessness and social care with FutureGov and Uscreates, and more recently five years as Lead UX Designer at Kaluza, leading design and research across a payments and credit product for a global SaaS platform.

I'm now bringing both strands back together as independent work — product and UX consulting, and creative workshops for wellbeing — grounded in the same belief either way: that good design starts with genuinely understanding people.
